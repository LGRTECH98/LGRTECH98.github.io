Our story

Good coffee brings people together.

We believe the best places are the ones that make you want to slow down. A place to catch up with a friend, finish a project, discover a new favourite drink or simply enjoy a quiet moment.

Inspired by Kenya’s rich coffee culture, Ken & LGR celebrates quality, hospitality and the everyday conversations that make life interesting.
